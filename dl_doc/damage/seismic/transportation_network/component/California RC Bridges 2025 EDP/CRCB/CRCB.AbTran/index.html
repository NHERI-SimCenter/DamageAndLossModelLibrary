CRCB.AbTran - Transverse abutment

The following models are available:

CRCB.AbTran.MultiSpan.M17 | Abutment fragility in terms of peak abutment transverse displacement for multi-span bridges of any era.

DesignSafe PRJ-5910 v1, EDP_Fragility Database.csv, data row 12 (spreadsheet row 14), AbTran, EDP “Displacement (in.)”; source reference “All six listed in Table 3 of the paper”.
The model gives the probability of reaching or exceeding each damage state as a lognormal function of the peak response of the component. The demand comes from a structural analysis of the bridge. The model uses the median capacity of the source and the dispersion of 0.35 that the database assigns to every EDP-based model. Each capacity applies to one unit of the component, and the demand is the response of that unit. The demand is directional (Demand-Directional 1), so it is supplied in the direction in which the EDP is defined, as a demand of type ABT at the location and direction of the component. These models and the Sa(1.0 s)-based models of seismic/transportation_network/component/California RC Bridges 2025 and seismic/transportation_network/portfolio/California RC Bridges 2025 are alternative representations of the same damage; apply only one of them to a given bridge. The EDP definition follows Mangalathu (2017) Sec. 6.1 and Table 6.1.
The capacities are those of Ramanathan (2012) Tables 5.14 and 5.17 as adopted by Mangalathu (2017) Table 6.13. The database assigns these capacities to all six multi-span studies listed in Chen et al. (2025, Table 3).

Suggested Block Size: 1 EA (round up to integer quantity)
