CRCB.E123.S1.C0.S - seat abutments

The following models are available:

CRCB.E123.S1.C0.S.AbAct.Slab.CPR22 | Active abutment fragility for a single-span (any era) bridge with no bents and seat abutments. Model attributes: slab deck.

DesignSafe PRJ-5910 v1, Sa_1_Fragility Database.csv, data row 28 (spreadsheet row 30), group E123-S1-C0-S, AbPass; source note “Slab deck”. The curve describes the most-damaged unit of this component type in the bridge (the source analyses record one peak demand per component type), so assign a quantity of one per bridge. Curves for the same component and group from different sources are alternatives, and the database publishes no weights for choosing among them. The component curves do not carry the demand correlation behind the system curves. The component curves and the system curve of the same group (seismic/transportation_network/portfolio/California RC Bridges 2025) are two representations of the same bridge. Apply one or the other to a given bridge, because applying both to the same bridge double-counts its damage; different bridges in one regional assessment may use different representations.
Source study: Cavalcante et al. (2022), a study of typical bridges in northeastern Brazil, including single-span T-beam and slab-deck bridges with seat abutments. Chen et al. (2025) use these curves as a proxy for California single-span bridges. They found no era-specific fragility models for single-span bridges, so the E123 groups apply to bridges of any era. The horizontal-component definition and the spectral damping of Sa(1.0 s) are not stated.
The source lists this curve as AbPass; its values are those that Cavalcante et al. (2022, Table 9) give for the active abutment, so it is stored as AbAct.

Suggested Block Size: 1 EA (round up to integer quantity)


Cavalcante, G. H. F., E. M. V. Pereira, I. D. Rodrigues, L. C. M. Vieira Junior, J. E. Padgett, and G. H. Siqueira. 2022. Seismic fragility assessment of typical bridges in Northeastern Brazil. Latin American Journal of Solids and Structures, 19 (5): e457. https://doi.org/10.1590/1679-78257062.
Chen, S., Y. Xie, C. Wu, H. V. Burton, J. E. Padgett, and Á. Zsarnóczay. 2025. Second-generation component and system-level seismic fragility models for reinforced concrete bridges in California. Earthquake Spectra, 41 (4): 3234–3253. https://doi.org/10.1177/87552930251343634.
