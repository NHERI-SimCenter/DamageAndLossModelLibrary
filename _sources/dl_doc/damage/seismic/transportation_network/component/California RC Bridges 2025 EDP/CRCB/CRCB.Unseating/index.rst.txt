CRCB.Unseating - Span unseating
*******************************

The following models are available:


.. raw:: html

   <p class="dl_comp_name"><b>CRCB.Unseating.E1.M17</b> | Span unseating fragility in terms of peak joint opening for Era 1 (pre-1970) multi-span bridges.</p>
   <div>

| DesignSafe PRJ-5910 v1, EDP_Fragility Database.csv, data row 7 (spreadsheet row 9), Unseating, EDP "Displacement (in.)"; source note "For Era 1 bridges"; source reference "All six listed in Table 3 of the paper, except for Mangalathu, Soleimani, et al. (2017)".
| The model gives the probability of reaching or exceeding each damage state as a lognormal function of the peak response of the component. The demand comes from a structural analysis of the bridge. The model uses the median capacity of the source and the dispersion of 0.35 that the database assigns to every EDP-based model. Each capacity applies to one unit of the component, and the demand is the response of that unit. The demand is directional (Demand-Directional 1), so it is supplied in the direction in which the EDP is defined, as a demand of type JOP at the location and direction of the component. These models and the Sa(1.0 s)-based models of seismic/transportation_network/component/California RC Bridges 2025 and seismic/transportation_network/portfolio/California RC Bridges 2025 are alternative representations of the same damage; apply only one of them to a given bridge. The EDP definition follows Mangalathu (2017) Sec. 6.1 and Table 6.1.
| The capacities are the four-state joint-opening capacities for Era 1 bridges of Mangalathu (2017) Table 6.13 and Soleimani (2017) Table 7.5, which Mangalathu (2017, p. 129) developed from Ramanathan (2012) and discussion with Caltrans engineers. The database assigns them to five of the six multi-span studies listed in Chen et al. (2025, Table 3), all but Mangalathu, Soleimani, and Jeon (2017). Mangalathu et al. (2018, Table 2) define unseating capacity for the extensive and complete states only, and their Era 1 values equal those of this set.


Suggested Block Size: 1 EA (round up to integer quantity)
