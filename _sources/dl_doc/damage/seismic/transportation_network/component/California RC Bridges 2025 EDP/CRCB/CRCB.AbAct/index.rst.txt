

****************************
CRCB.AbAct - Active abutment
****************************

The following models are available:


.. raw:: html

   <p class="dl_comp_name"><b>CRCB.AbAct.MultiSpan.M17</b> | Abutment fragility in terms of peak abutment active displacement for multi-span bridges of any era.</p>
   <div>

| DesignSafe PRJ-5910 v1, EDP_Fragility Database.csv, data row 10 (spreadsheet row 12), AbAct, EDP "Displacement (in.)"; source reference "All six listed in Table 3 of the paper".
| The model gives the probability of reaching or exceeding each damage state as a lognormal function of the peak response of the component. The demand comes from a structural analysis of the bridge. The model uses the median capacity of the source and the dispersion of 0.35 that the database assigns to every EDP-based model. Each capacity applies to one unit of the component, and the demand is the response of that unit. The demand is directional (Demand-Directional 1), so it is supplied in the direction in which the EDP is defined, as a demand of type ABA at the location and direction of the component. These models and the Sa(1.0 s)-based models of seismic/transportation_network/component/California RC Bridges 2025 and seismic/transportation_network/portfolio/California RC Bridges 2025 are alternative representations of the same damage; apply only one of them to a given bridge. The EDP definition follows Mangalathu (2017) Sec. 6.1 and Table 6.1.
| The capacities are those of Ramanathan (2012) Tables 5.14 and 5.17 as adopted by Mangalathu (2017) Table 6.13. The database assigns these capacities to all six multi-span studies listed in Chen et al. (2025, Table 3).


Suggested Block Size: 1 EA (round up to integer quantity)
