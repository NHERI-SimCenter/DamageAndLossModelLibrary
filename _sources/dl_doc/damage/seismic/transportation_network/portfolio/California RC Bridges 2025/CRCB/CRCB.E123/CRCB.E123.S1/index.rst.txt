   <p class="dl_comp_name"><b>CRCB.E123.S1.C0.D.System.Slab.Mod1.CPR22</b> | Bridge system fragility for a single-span (any era) bridge with no bents and diaphragm abutments. Model attributes: slab deck, modification factors from Era 1 two-span, single-column bridges.</p>
   <div>

| DesignSafe PRJ-5910 v1, Sa_1_Fragility Database.csv, data row 18 (spreadsheet row 20), group E123-S1-C0-D, System; source note "Slab deck, modification factors computed by using Era 1 bridge fragilities". The curve is a series-system fragility that the source study derived from correlated component demands. This curve and the component curves of the same group (seismic/transportation_network/component/California RC Bridges 2025) are two representations of the same bridge. Apply one or the other to a given bridge, because applying both to the same bridge double-counts its damage; different bridges in one regional assessment may use different representations.
| Source study: Cavalcante et al. (2022), a study of typical bridges in northeastern Brazil, including single-span T-beam and slab-deck bridges with seat abutments. Chen et al. (2025) use these curves as a proxy for California single-span bridges. They found no era-specific fragility models for single-span bridges, so the E123 groups apply to bridges of any era. The horizontal-component definition and the spectral damping of Sa(1.0 s) are not stated. In the literature they surveyed, Chen et al. (2025) found no viable set of fragility models for single-span bridges with diaphragm abutments. They derived this curve from the seat-abutment curve with modification factors, the ratios of the diaphragm-abutment to the seat-abutment fragility parameters of the two-span, single-column groups of the named era. The published values show the factors applied to the medians and the dispersions; the paper describes the adjustment of the medians only.
| Extensive and complete damage are not defined for single-span bridges with diaphragm abutments, which have no seats or columns.
