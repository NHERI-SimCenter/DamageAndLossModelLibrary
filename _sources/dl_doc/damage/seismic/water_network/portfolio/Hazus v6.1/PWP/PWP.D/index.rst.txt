

*********************
PWP.D - Ductile pipes
*********************

The following models are available:


.. raw:: html

   <p class="dl_comp_name"><b>PWP.D.GF</b> | Ductile potable water pipe (ductile iron, PVC, and steel other than the brittle cases) under ground failure.</p>
   <div>

| Hazus repair rate 0.3 × PGD^0.56 repairs per km for a liquefaction probability of 1 (Table 8-12, PWP2), multiplied by a 20 ft (0.006096 km) segment length and capped at 1.


Suggested Block Size: 1 EA (round up to integer quantity)
