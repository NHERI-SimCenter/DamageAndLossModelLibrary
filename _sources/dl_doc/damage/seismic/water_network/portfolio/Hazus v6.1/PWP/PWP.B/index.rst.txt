

*********************
PWP.B - Brittle pipes
*********************

The following models are available:


.. raw:: html

   <p class="dl_comp_name"><b>PWP.B.GF</b> | Brittle potable water pipe (cast iron, asbestos cement, concrete, gas-welded steel, or pre-1935 steel of unknown weld type) under ground failure.</p>
   <div>

| Hazus repair rate PGD^0.56 repairs per km for a liquefaction probability of 1 (Table 8-12, PWP1), multiplied by a 20 ft (0.006096 km) segment length and capped at 1.


Suggested Block Size: 1 EA (round up to integer quantity)
